Deployment

Sovereign deployment models

Every Active Mirror deployment is sovereign by default. We offer three models — choose based on your threat model, regulatory requirements, and infrastructure constraints. In all cases, your data stays inside your compliance boundary.

Sovereign Deployment

Your data never leaves your boundary.

Three deployment models. All sovereign. Choose based on your threat model, not our convenience.

Air-Gapped

Complete network isolation. Models, data, and inference run entirely within your physical boundary. No external calls, no telemetry, no cloud dependency.

  • Zero network egress
  • On-premise hardware
  • Full data sovereignty
  • HSM key management

Single-Tenant VPC

Dedicated cloud infrastructure with no shared resources. Your models run in isolated compute with private networking and customer-managed encryption keys.

  • Dedicated compute
  • Private networking
  • CMEK encryption
  • SOC 2 boundary

Local Edge Inference

Run quantised models on commodity hardware at the edge. Branch offices, factory floors, field deployments. Sub-10ms latency with no cloud round-trip.

  • Quantised models
  • ARM / x86 support
  • Offline-capable
  • < 10ms latency
