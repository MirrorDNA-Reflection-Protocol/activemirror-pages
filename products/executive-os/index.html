Products / Executive AI Operating Layer

Personal AI for executives, founders, and serious operators.

Active Mirror can build a private assistant layer around the way a senior operator actually works: briefings, research, reminders, documents, decisions, delegated tasks, and follow-through. The system is designed with boundaries, approvals, and evidence from the beginning.

This is positioned as an operating layer, not a novelty assistant. It is useful when personal context, business workflows, and trusted delegation need to be managed together.

Briefings and Research

Daily briefs, market scans, stakeholder notes, document review, source-backed summaries, and structured research packets.

Task Follow-Through

Action tracking, priority queues, reminders, handoffs, escalation prompts, and status views across active workstreams.

Personal Knowledge Interface

A controlled interface for asking questions across approved notes, files, project context, public sources, and operating records.

Monitors and Alerts

Watchlists for sites, services, launches, inbox signals, deadlines, documents, operational checks, and strategic topics.

Delegated Workflows

Scoped actions such as drafting, triage, research, route checks, status updates, and prepared next steps with review points.

Approvals and Evidence

Human approval gates, action receipts, source links, operating logs, and boundaries for what the assistant may do autonomously.
