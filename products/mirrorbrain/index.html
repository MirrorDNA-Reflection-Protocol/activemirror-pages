MirrorBrain

Local-first inference engine

MirrorBrain runs quantised models on-premise or at the edge with deployment options designed for controlled data boundaries. Inference can be configured near the systems where the data lives.

Supports ARM and x86 architectures. Runs on commodity hardware — from rack servers to edge devices. Models are optimised against the latency, cost, privacy, and reliability constraints of the target environment.

Built for organisations with strict data residency requirements, air-gapped environments, or edge deployments where latency and sovereignty are non-negotiable.
