Services / AI Consulting and Advisory

End-to-end AI consulting for strategy, governance, implementation, and operation.

Active Mirror advises organizations that need practical AI decisions: what to build, what to buy, what to govern, what to automate, and how to move from strategy to controlled operation without separating advisory from delivery.

Advisory work can stand alone or lead into a scoped build. The output is designed to be useful to leadership, technical teams, operations, compliance, and procurement. For qualified opportunities, advisory can define a bounded pilot before the work expands.

AI Strategy and Use-Case Selection

Identify where AI should be used, where human review should remain, what to build first, and how to sequence the work against business value.

Architecture Review

Assess model routing, data boundaries, cloud versus local deployment, integration surfaces, observability, security controls, and operating ownership.

Governance and Guardrail Design

Define policies, approval gates, logs, model cards, evidence requirements, review paths, and operating controls for production AI systems.

Automation Discovery

Map repeatable work, decision points, data sources, handoffs, exceptions, and the smallest practical path to a working automation demo.

Implementation Oversight

Support build teams with scope, technical review, vendor coordination, delivery checkpoints, launch readiness, and post-launch operating controls.

Executive Enablement

Brief leadership teams on AI adoption, operating controls, internal workflows, realistic timelines, procurement questions, and board-level decision points.
