About Active Mirror

The shift from prediction to reflection.

What we believe

AI should be verifiable, not trusted on faith. Every AI output should carry proof of where it came from, what rules governed it, and whether a human reviewed it. We build the infrastructure that makes this possible.

Who we are

Active Mirror is built by Paul Desai, founder of N1 Intelligence (OPC) Pvt Ltd, from Goa, India. Started in early 2025, the company builds verifiable AI products — from free scam protection for Indian consumers to governed inference infrastructure for enterprises. No VC funding. No board. Just working product.

What we've built

  • Chetana — Free scam checks in 12 Indian languages, processing entirely on-device
  • MirrorGate — Self-hosted AI inference proxy with safety, redaction, and compliance enforcement
  • Mirror Seed — Portable, sovereign identity files you own and carry between AI tools
  • MirrorProd — AI-powered professional video production without shoots
