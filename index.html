Frequently Asked Questions

What is Active Mirror?

Active Mirror builds verifiable AI products. Our flagship product Chetana helps people check suspicious messages, links, UPI requests, and phone numbers before acting on them. For enterprises, MirrorGate provides governed AI inference with full audit trails.

Is Chetana really free?

Yes. Chetana scam checks are free, unlimited, and require no account. We believe scam protection is a public good, not a premium feature.

Does Active Mirror store my data?

No. Chetana runs entirely on your device. Your messages, screenshots, and UPI details never leave your phone. There is no server to breach because we never collect it.

Who is Active Mirror for?

Individuals protecting themselves from digital fraud. Teams and enterprises who need governed, auditable AI inference. Schools and educators who need review-ready AI outputs. Anyone who believes AI should be verified before trusted.

How is this different from other AI safety tools?

Most AI safety tools audit models after the fact. Active Mirror operates at the point of action — before you send money, before you click a link, before an AI response reaches production. We verify the moment that matters.

What languages does Chetana support?

Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, and Assamese. More languages are in development.
